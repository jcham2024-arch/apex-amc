# 01 — Feed and API options

Task `retailer-price-data-options`. **Researched 3 Oct 2026.**

The brief asked which lawful routes exist. **The single most important finding is that
the most obvious one is not available to us at all**, and the reason is structural rather
than legal.

---

## 1. AmmoSeek's XML feed — the direction everyone gets backwards

**AmmoSeek publishes a feed specification. It is a specification for RETAILERS TO FEED
AMMOSEEK. It is not a feed AmmoSeek offers to consumers.**

From `https://ammoseek.com/xml/`, the feed is what *"AmmoSeek needs … in order to list
your products"*:

> "In order to list your products, AmmoSeek needs a data feed (in XML or JSON format)
> which describes all of your in-stock products … It should reside on a web server where
> **we** can access it at any time."

And the ACR signup form requires **the retailer** to supply it:

> "Section 3: Data Feed Information — **Data Feed URL** … Feed must be in XML or JSON
> format per our specification"

**Consequence: we cannot subscribe to it.** The feed runs retailer → AmmoSeek. Joining
the ACR programme means becoming a *seller* and getting our own catalogue listed — not
gaining access to our competitors' prices. **This is not a data source for us at all.**

### What the feed contains (for the record)

| Field | Present | Note |
|---|---|---|
| `<price>` | **Yes** | Required for every record |
| `<qty_available>` / `<availability>` | **Yes** | Stock state |
| `<upc>`, `<mpn>` | Yes, "when they exist" | The join keys |
| `<numrounds>` / `<count>` | Yes | Per-price quantity |
| `<caliber>` | Yes | For ammo, bullets, brass, magazines |
| `<brand>` | Yes | "the brand name most prominently displayed on the box" |

### Requirements that matter if we ever ran a feed

> "The feed must be **real-time or updated at least every 5 minutes**."
> "The information in your data feed **must match your website at all times**."

AmmoSeek also **explicitly rejects third-party feed generators**, naming DataFeedWatch
(60-minute refresh) and FeedGeni (1–2 hour refresh) as unusable — which tells us the
5-minute standard is real and enforced.

**Cost to a retailer:** the ACR form requires business registration details, owner name
and date of birth for identity verification, and a technical contact. No fee is
advertised on the signup page. **Cost is primarily the engineering and the 5-minute
SLA.**

## 2. Gunfinder — a European marketplace, not a US product feed

The brief lists Gunfinder as a feed specification provider. **Measured, it is not that.**

`https://gunfinder.com/` returns a consumer marketplace: *"450,000+ listings · 450 dealers
& partners"*, priced in **euros**, with **used firearms, classifieds and auctions** as
first-class sections.

- Locale switcher: German, English, Dutch, Polish, Italian, French — **Europe-first.**
- Sample listings: `1,295 €`, `2,200 €`, `450 €`.
- Categories include `Blank`, `Airsoft`, `Paintball` — a European/EU market profile.

**No product data feed is offered to publishers on any page I could reach.** The
affiliates URL 404s. **It does not appear to carry our US reloading retailers at all.**

**Verdict: not a source for US ammunition pricing.** Recorded rather than assumed, since
the brief asserted otherwise.

## 3. DOPE — the domain is parked

The brief lists DOPE as publishing a feed specification. **Measured 3 Oct 2026:**

```
https://www.dope.com/  -> "DOPE – Opening Soon ... Coming Soon"
                          "POS and Ecommerce by Shopify"
                          "Enter store using password"
```

**It is a Shopify store behind a coming-soon page and a password.** Not an active
retailer, not a feed provider. The `/affiliates` path returns the same parked page.

**The brief is out of date on this one.** Recorded, not silently dropped.

## 4. Affiliate networks — the route that actually works

### AvantLink

**Confirmed from `https://www.avantlink.com/publishers/`**, and the critical detail:

> "**Data Feed Manager:** Customizable merchant product feeds that are updated **daily**"
> "**API:** Advanced data input and retrieval functionality for affiliates"

Also confirmed: *"You will earn a commission from sales generated by referrals"*, 2,000+
advertisers, US/Canada/Australia networks.

**The feed cadence is stated as daily.** That is not a footnote — it is the number that
decides the staleness question in `03-staleness-and-display.md`.

**Not confirmed, and must not be assumed:** whether AvantLink carries our specific
retailers. The project README names AvantLink but records that *"several programs have
since migrated"*, so `sourcing_retailers.csv` correctly leaves `affiliate_network` NULL.
**Confirming per-retailer affiliation is an account-holder action, not a research one.**

### Impact / Awin

Both operate affiliate networks and both historically provide product feeds to approved
affiliates. **I could not retrieve either's feed documentation** — the Impact help URL
404'd, and Awin's is behind an application wall.

**So: stated as widely-reported to exist, NOT verified here.** Per `.clinerules` §5
rule 1, an unverified value stays EMPTY. These are recorded as *candidates to verify
after joining*, not as confirmed options.

## Summary

| Route | Gives price+stock? | Cost | Needs retailer? | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **AmmoSeek XML** | Yes — but **retailer→AmmoSeek** | n/a | **Yes — we must be the seller** | **Not available to us** |
| **Gunfinder** | No feed found; EUR marketplace | — | — | **Not a US source** |
| **DOPE** | — | — | — | **Parked domain** |
| **AvantLink** | Yes, **feeds updated daily** | Commission share | Yes, per merchant | **Recommended** |
| **Impact / Awin** | Reported, **unverified** | Commission share | Yes | Verify after joining |
| **Scraping** | Yes | Free | **No** | **See `02-scraping-legality.md`** |

**One of four named routes exists as described. Two do not. The fourth is unverified.**
That is the finding, and it is why `02-scraping-legality.md` matters: with the legitimate
routes this thin, scraping will be proposed again, and the answer has to be on record.
