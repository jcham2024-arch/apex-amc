# in-progress/

**Yours.** A claimed task lives here while you work on it.

Read [../README.md](../README.md) first - it is binding. In short:

- Claim a task by MOVING its brief here from `pending/`. The move is the lock.
- Do your work here. Nothing in this folder is reviewed, gated, or committed.
- Write your `REPORT.md` and your staged copies in your own task folder.
- When done, move the whole folder to `../completed/`, then look for another task.

Untracked by design: it is mid-flight by definition, and a folder of half-written work
belongs to the agent that is writing it, not to git history.
