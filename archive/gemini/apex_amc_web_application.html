Knowledge Base

Handloading Fundamentals & Tips

Single-Stage vs. Progressive Press

Single-stage presses offer ultimate precision for rifle rounds (100 rnds/hr). Progressive presses produce up to 500+ pistol rounds per hour.

Smokeless Powder Burn Rates

Fast powders (e.g., Bullseye, Titegroup) work best in short handgun barrels. Slow powders (e.g., Varget, H4350) generate maximum velocity in long rifle barrels.

Managing Headspace & OAL

Overall Length (OAL) ensures reliable feeding from magazines and prevents dangerous pressure spikes caused by seating bullets too deep into the case neck.
